Our Approach

TWOCHAPTER is a Bali-based photography team capturing people, places, and moments through our own perspective. From personal portraits and couple sessions to sports and special events, we enjoy documenting stories in all their forms.

Behind every shoot is a team of photographers who share the same passion for creating meaningful images. We bring different perspectives and strengths together to make every experience special.

Our approach is simple: keep things natural, make you feel comfortable, and capture moments as they happen. We believe the best photographs are the ones that feel genuine.

— Ruben Juan Lesmana, TWOCHAPTER

Our Values

Slow

One session a day, wherever possible. Rushing is the fastest way to make a place look like a backdrop.

Honest

No forced smiles, no borrowed poses. We photograph what is actually happening, including the mess.

Yours

Private galleries, personal licences, and files you keep. Nothing is published without your permission.
